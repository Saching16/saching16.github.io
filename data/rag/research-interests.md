# Research Interests

Sachin is currently focused on agents and latent space reasoning, especially how multi-agent systems can reason, coordinate, and improve when part of their planning happens in latent representations instead of only natural language traces.

Current research directions:

- Agentic systems that combine planning, tool use, retrieval, memory, and self-evaluation.
- Latent space reasoning for more efficient and expressive multi-step inference.
- LatentMAS: multi-agent systems that coordinate through latent representations, shared state, or compressed reasoning signals.
- RecursiveMAS: recursive multi-agent systems where agents can decompose problems into sub-agent workflows, evaluate intermediate outputs, and refine strategy over multiple reasoning passes.
- Bridging research prototypes with production AI engineering, including observability, scalable retrieval, and robust backend infrastructure.
